# For showing the owner

`Bakers-Inn-walkthrough.html` — the animated walkthrough of one trading day.

One file, with nothing loaded from the internet: no fonts, no images, no
scripts from anywhere. Double-click it and it opens in any browser, **with the
wifi off**. That is deliberate — the meeting may not have a connection, and a
presentation that needs one is a presentation that fails.

Ten scenes, about a minute and a half, playing itself:

1. The three shops, and the shape of the business
2. Each shop orders at closing time
3. 05:00 — three orders become one baking list
4. The kitchen records what actually came out
5. The vans go out; each shop counts what arrived
6. The till: weighed items, off-list items, selling with no internet
7. Closing time: count the drawer, count the shelf
8. The daily sheet writing itself
9. What it needs from him

**Space** pauses, **←** **→** step, clicking anywhere advances. Pause on
whichever scene he asks about and talk from there.

It opens on his own daily sheet — the row from his pad for 13 August — because
that is the thing he recognises before anything else on the screen.

It deliberately does not mention price. That is a conversation, not a slide;
a number on the page invites him to weigh it before he has seen what he is
weighing.

It follows the same brief as the app itself — *minimalistically professional,
nothing flashy* — and uses the app's own palette, including its rule that red
means one thing only: money that does not add up.
